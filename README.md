# Council on Depollution (CODE)

Website for the Council on Depollution — an initiative focused on raising awareness of toxic chemicals in everyday consumer products and advising on healthier living practices.

## Sections

- **The Problem** — Research-backed overview of chemical exposure risks, including statistics on cord blood contamination, sperm count decline, endocrine disruptor costs, and public concern across Europe
- **About CODE** — Mission and services: awareness, lifestyle guidance, ingredient list interpretation, and work with industry on safer alternatives
- **Our Story** — Founders Eline Bakker and Ljiljana Rodic-Wiersma, their backgrounds and motivation
- **Toxin-Free Start to Life Guide** — Upcoming guide covering preconception through the first 1000 days, with links to community surveys (English and Dutch)
